TallyMobile / BankAlert – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

TallyMobile (including its Gmail reader, “BankAlert”) is a private tool used only by Shivam Hardware, Shivam Traders and Satyam Traders for their own accounting. It is not offered to the public.

What the app reads

With the account owner’s permission, the app has read-only access to the business’s own Gmail account. It reads only bank transaction alert emails and emails carrying purchase bills sent to that account.

How the information is used

Transaction details (date, amount, narration) and bill contents are used only to prepare accounting entries in the business’s own Tally accounts, which the owner reviews before anything is posted. The app never sends, deletes or changes emails.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone. It is stored only on the business’s own PC and in its own private Firebase database, accessible only to the business owner.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account’s security settings (“Third-party apps with account access”).
